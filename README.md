# Optical Audio Communication System

A bidirectional laser-based audio communication system designed and built using analog circuitry, optical transmitters, and receivers.

## Project Overview

This project focuses on transmitting audio signals through a laser-based optical link and converting the received signals back into audible sound through a speaker.

The circuit incorporates adjustable-gain amplification, audio signal mixing, LED signal-level indicators, and speaker amplification.

## Circuit Design

- **Optical Transmitter and Receiver:** Laser-based audio transmission and optical signal detection.
- **Amplification:** Adjustable-gain amplifier stages for controlling individual audio signals.
- **Audio Mixing:** Summing amplifier combining microphone, computer, and received optical audio inputs.
- **Signal Indicators:** Four-level LED indicators for monitoring audio signal levels.
- **Audio Output:** Amplifier designed to drive an 8 Ω speaker with a target output of 1 W.

## Simulation and Testing

Circuit stages were simulated in LTspice before being assembled and tested on breadboards. Hardware testing demonstrated successful laser-based audio transmission and speaker playback.
